Before You Order

Terms & Conditions

A few things worth knowing before you place your order.

  1. Advance PaymentTo confirm your order, a minimum of 50% or full payment is required in advance. Thank you for understanding!
  2. Pick-up & DeliverySelf pick-up is highly preferred. Delivery is also available via a third-party service (InDrive) at the customer's risk, and we can share the rider's contact details with you.
  3. Booking NoticeConfirm standard orders at least 2–3 days prior, and larger orders 5 days in advance.
  4. Urgent OrdersLast-minute or urgent orders are purely subject to slot availability.
  5. Cake CarePick up your order on time and refrigerate it immediately. Cream melts quickly in hot temperatures!
  6. Design VariationsCustomer-provided images are used as inspiration only; final colors and designs may vary slightly.
  7. CancellationsOrder cancellations must be requested 24 hours in advance. Orders already in progress cannot be cancelled.
  8. Dark ColorsDeep or dark icing colors can leave stains and may naturally have a slightly bitter taste.
  9. Fixed PricesOur prices are non-negotiable. However, custom discounted packages can be arranged for large parties or events.
